The problem

Automatic translation is not localization.

And synthetic voice alone is not communication.

Most AI dubbing tools can generate speech. But they often miss what actually matters:

  • cultural context;
  • rhythm;
  • pronunciation;
  • emotional intention;
  • terminology;
  • timing;
  • sonic quality;
  • trust.

For Brazilian audiences, these details decide whether a video feels native or instantly disposable.

Market opportunity

Brazil is not just another language checkbox.

Brazilian Portuguese is one of the largest digital language markets in the world, with a massive audience consuming education, podcasts, product videos, training, institutional communication and entertainment online.

But entering Brazil requires more than subtitles. It requires voice, rhythm, culture and trust.

Workflow

From content to publish-ready localized voice.

01

Understand

02

03

AI voices are created, tested and refined with human direction.

04

Review

05

Finish

06
