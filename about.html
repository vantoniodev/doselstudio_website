About Dosel

Built between systems, sound and language.

Dosel Studio is a Brazilian audio and audiovisual studio focused on cultural localization, human-directed AI voices and professional studio finishing.

The studio was founded by Vinicius Antonio, whose career began in 2001 inside the infrastructure layers of enterprise computing: databases, Linux, Cisco, SAP, networking, security and GRC across complex IT environments in Brazil.

In parallel, Vinicius built a second path through synthesis, acoustics, sound design and studio production. This became VAntônio, a live electronic music project centered on hardware, modular synthesis and a deep relationship with sound.

In 2015, he founded MIDITerapia, an education initiative created to expand access to electronic music production and audio engineering knowledge in Brazil.

In 2018, he moved fully into professional audio, building a hybrid analog/digital studio and working across mixing, mastering and post-production. Since 2023, this research has expanded into AI voice systems, LLMs, TTS engines, Python-based pipelines and voice processing.

Dosel Studio was created from that intersection. Its first product, Vox-Humana, is a cultural localization workflow for Brazilian Portuguese, combining AI-generated voices, human direction, cultural review, voice processing and professional audio finishing.

It is not a commodity AI tool. It is a craft-informed system built from both engineering and listening. The same thread connects all of this work: understanding systems from the inside, whether the system is enterprise infrastructure, an analog signal chain or an AI-powered audio pipeline.
